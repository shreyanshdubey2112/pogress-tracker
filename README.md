# Google Learn with Communities — Skill Badges & Milestones Tracker

A tracker designed for **Google Learn with Communities** and **GDG on Campus DCRUST** to verify participant progress on Google Cloud Skills Boost.

Learners enter their public profile link, and the system verifies how many skill badges out of the official **10 fixed cohort curriculum** they have completed, unlocking milestone goodies:
- **Milestone 1 (5 Badges)**: Official Google Water Bottle + Certificate.
- **Milestone 2 (10 Badges)**: Google Developers Kit & Google T-Shirt + Completion Certificate.

---

## 🌟 Features

1. **Live Profile Verification:**
   - Accepts public profile URLs from `skills.google` or `cloudskillsboost.google`.
   - Automatically detects completed badges and matches them against the fixed 10 badge curriculum.
   - Shows badge count, percentage, progress bar, and earned dates.

2. **Milestone Goodies:**
   - **Milestone 1 (5 Badges)**: Showcases the Google Water Bottle with unlocked celebration status.
   - **Milestone 2 (10 Badges)**: Showcases the Google Developers Kit & Google T-Shirt with confetti celebration.

3. **Fixed 10 Skill Badges Curriculum:**
   1. **The Basics of Google Cloud Compute** (4 Credits) — [Course Link](https://www.skills.google/course_templates/754)
   2. **Configure Service Accounts and IAM Roles for Google Cloud** (4 Credits) — [Course Link](https://www.skills.google/course_templates/702)
   3. **Implement Speech and Language Solutions with Pre-trained APIs** (4 Credits) — [Course Link](https://www.skills.google/course_templates/700)
   4. **Develop AI-Powered Prototypes in Google AI Studio** (0 Credits / Free) — [Course Link](https://www.skills.google/course_templates/1426)
   5. **Analyze Images with the Cloud Vision API** (12 Credits) — [Course Link](https://www.skills.google/course_templates/633)
   6. **Build a Website on Google Cloud** (13 Credits) — [Course Link](https://www.skills.google/course_templates/638)
   7. **Create Your First Gemini Enterprise Application** (0 Credits / Free) — [Course Link](https://www.skills.google/course_templates/1586/)
   8. **Store, Process, and Manage Data on Google Cloud - Console** (3 Credits) — [Course Link](https://www.skills.google/course_templates/658)
   9. **Using the Google Cloud Speech API** (4 Credits) — [Course Link](https://www.skills.google/course_templates/756)
   10. **Explore Generative AI in Agent Platform** (16 Credits) — [Course Link](https://www.skills.google/course_templates/959)

4. **Direct Course Links:**
   - Learners can click "Start Badge ↗" on any pending badge to open the exact lab template on `skills.google`.

